(PC)
1. Make a Unity Project
2. Import VRC SDK
3. Import Dynamic Bones (optional)
4. Import Marocchino.unitypackage
5. Open Scene Assets/CaféMohka/Marocchino/MarocchinoScene.unity
5a. In the middle you find a Maro version with all Meshes on her.
5b. She has shapekeys for her face and her body to hide certain body parts to ensure nothing is clipping.
6. Pick a model from under "Upload Ready" section.
7. Hit Upload on the VRC SDK Panel.
8. Have fun!


(Quest/Fallback)
1. The Models in the Scene with the postfix "Quest" have the correct Quest compatible shaders applied. You may import those into your Android Project.
2. Follow: https://docs.vrchat.com/docs/avatar-fallback-system
